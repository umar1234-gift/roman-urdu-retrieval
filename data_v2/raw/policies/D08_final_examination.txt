# Final Examination

Document ID: D08
Policy Area: Final Examination

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Final Examination policy defines the passing criteria for all end-of-semester examinations.

To pass a final examination, a student must secure a minimum of 50% of the total marks. Failure to meet this passing threshold results in a failing grade for the course.

The 50% threshold applies uniformly across all departments. Supplementary examinations are offered only to students who narrowly miss the pass mark.
