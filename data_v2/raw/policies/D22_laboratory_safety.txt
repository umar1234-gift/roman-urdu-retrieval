# Laboratory Safety

Document ID: D22
Policy Area: Laboratory Safety

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Laboratory Safety policy establishes mandatory precautions for all students working in university laboratories.

Safety goggles must be worn at all times inside the laboratory. Students without protective equipment are not permitted to enter or work in the lab.

Additional protective equipment such as gloves and lab coats may be required depending on the experiment. Any accident must be reported to the lab supervisor immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Laboratory Safety policy.
