# Course Add and Drop

Document ID: D02
Policy Area: Course Add and Drop

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Course Add and Drop policy defines the enrollment adjustment period during which students may modify their registered courses without academic penalty.

Students are permitted to add or drop a course within 7 working days of the semester commencement. After this enrollment adjustment window closes, any change requires formal approval from the department.

The drop window is strictly enforced. Late requests are only considered in documented medical emergencies and must be routed through the registrar's office.

End of Course Add and Drop policy.
