# Graduation Eligibility

Document ID: D11
Policy Area: Graduation Eligibility

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Graduation Eligibility policy defines the minimum academic standing required for degree conferral.

A student must achieve a minimum CGPA of 2.00 to be eligible for graduation. The CGPA threshold applies to the cumulative grade point average across all semesters.

Students whose CGPA falls below the graduation standing requirement must improve their grades through re-registration before applying for degree conferral.
