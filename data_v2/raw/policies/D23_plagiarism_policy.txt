# Plagiarism Policy

Document ID: D23
Policy Area: Plagiarism Policy

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Plagiarism Policy establishes the university's stance on academic dishonesty and the penalties for integrity violations.

Any confirmed case of plagiarism results in zero marks for the submitted work. Repeat offenses lead to suspension for one full academic semester.

All written assignments are screened through plagiarism-detection software. The integrity violation record is kept in the student's permanent academic file.
