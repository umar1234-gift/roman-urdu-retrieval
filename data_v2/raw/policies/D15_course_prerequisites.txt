# Course Prerequisites

Document ID: D15
Policy Area: Course Prerequisites

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Course Prerequisites policy defines the minimum grade required to satisfy prerequisite requirements for advanced courses.

A student must obtain at least a C grade in a prerequisite course to qualify for the dependent course. Prerequisite clearance is enforced automatically during registration.

Grades below the prerequisite grade threshold require the student to repeat the course before enrolling in the advanced course. Waivers are granted only in exceptional cases.
