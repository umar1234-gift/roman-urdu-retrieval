# Admission Application

Document ID: D03
Policy Area: Admission Application

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Admission Application policy governs the procedure and timeline for prospective students applying to undergraduate programs.

For the Fall 2026 admission cycle, the application cutoff for the Software Engineering program is 18 November 2026 at 11:59 PM. The submission portal closes precisely at this moment.

Applications received after the cutoff are automatically rejected by the system. No extensions are granted under any circumstances, including technical difficulties.

End of Admission Application policy.
