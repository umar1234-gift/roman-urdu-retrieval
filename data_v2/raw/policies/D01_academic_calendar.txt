# Academic Calendar

Document ID: D01
Policy Area: Academic Calendar

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Academic Calendar governs all scheduling matters for the university. It defines the commencement of each academic term, the duration of instructional weeks, and the official semester kickoff date.

For the Fall 2026 academic term, the official commencement date for all classes is 15 September 2026. No classes will be held before this date under any circumstances.

The academic term start is followed by a full sixteen-week instructional period. Deans and department heads are responsible for communicating any local scheduling adjustments to their faculty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Academic Calendar policy.
