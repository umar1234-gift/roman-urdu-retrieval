# Merit Scholarship

Document ID: D17
Policy Area: Merit Scholarship

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Merit Scholarship policy governs the award of tuition waivers based on academic performance.

To qualify for the merit award, a student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 3.50. The scholarship eligibility is reviewed at the end of every semester.

Recipients of the tuition waiver must maintain continuous full-time enrollment. Any drop in CGPA below the scholarship eligibility threshold results in immediate termination of the award.
