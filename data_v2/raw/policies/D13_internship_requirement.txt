# Internship Requirement

Document ID: D13
Policy Area: Internship Requirement

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Internship Requirement policy outlines the industrial training component required for degree completion.

Every student must complete a minimum internship duration of 6 weeks in an approved organization. The work placement must be documented and signed by the industrial supervisor.

The industrial training is typically undertaken during the summer break. Students must submit their internship report to the department within two weeks of completing the 6 weeks duration.
