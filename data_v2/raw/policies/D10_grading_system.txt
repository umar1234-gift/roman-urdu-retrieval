# Grading System

Document ID: D10
Policy Area: Grading System

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Grading System policy converts numeric scores into letter grades based on predefined boundaries.

To receive an A grade in any course, a student must obtain a grade boundary of at least 90% of the total marks. Scores between 85% and 89% fall into the A- category.

The letter grade scale is applied consistently across all semesters. Grade disputes must be raised with the instructor within one week of result announcement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Grading System policy.
