# Assignment Submission

Document ID: D04
Policy Area: Assignment Submission

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Assignment Submission policy specifies the rules for submitting coursework and the penalties for late submissions.

Students who miss the primary deadline are granted a late submission window of 3 calendar days. During this grace period, a fixed penalty of 10% per day is applied to the final grade.

After the late submission window expires, the assignment receives a grade of zero. Instructors may not extend this period without written approval from the dean.
