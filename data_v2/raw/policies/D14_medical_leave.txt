# Medical Leave

Document ID: D14
Policy Area: Medical Leave

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Medical Leave policy specifies the maximum health absence permitted for students during a semester.

A student may claim up to 10 days of medical leave per semester. Sick leave beyond this limit requires approval from the university medical board.

All health absence claims must be supported by a doctor's certificate. Medical leave days do not count against the regular attendance calculation.
