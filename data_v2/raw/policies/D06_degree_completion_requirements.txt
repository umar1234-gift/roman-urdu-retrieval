# Degree Completion Requirements

Document ID: D06
Policy Area: Degree Completion Requirements

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Degree Completion Requirements policy outlines the total academic load required for the award of a bachelor's degree.

To qualify for graduation, a student must successfully complete 136 credit hours of coursework. This includes core courses, electives, and the final year project.

The degree audit is conducted in the final semester. Students who fall short of the required credit hours must register for additional courses in the following term.
