# Research Thesis

Document ID: D27
Policy Area: Research Thesis

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Research Thesis policy defines the requirements for the final year project undertaken by undergraduate students.

The research component carries a total weight of 6 credit hours, spread across the final two semesters. The thesis credits count toward the total degree requirement.

Students must submit a written thesis and defend it before a panel. The final year project grade is assigned jointly by the supervisor and the examination panel.
