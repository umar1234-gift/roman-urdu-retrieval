# Class Attendance

Document ID: D05
Policy Area: Class Attendance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Class Attendance policy establishes the minimum presence required for a student to remain in good academic standing.

Every student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course. Falling below this attendance threshold results in automatic disqualification from the final examination.

Attendance is recorded per class session. Medical absences with documented proof are counted separately and do not count against the student's attendance percentage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Class Attendance policy.
