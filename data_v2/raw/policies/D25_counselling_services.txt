# Counselling Services

Document ID: D25
Policy Area: Counselling Services

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Counselling Services policy describes the mental health support available to enrolled students.

Each student is entitled to 8 sessions per semester with a professional counsellor at no cost. Student counselling sessions are confidential and cannot be shared without consent.

Students requiring extended mental health support beyond the 8 sessions may be referred to external specialists. All counselling records are kept separate from academic files.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Counselling Services policy.
