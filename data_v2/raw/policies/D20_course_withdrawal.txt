# Course Withdrawal

Document ID: D20
Policy Area: Course Withdrawal

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Course Withdrawal policy defines the window during which a student may drop a course without academic penalty.

A student may withdraw from a course within 30 days of the semester start without academic penalty. The withdrawal period applies only to elective courses.

After the penalty-free drop window closes, any course cancellation appears as a W grade on the transcript. Withdrawals from core courses require advisor approval regardless of timing.
