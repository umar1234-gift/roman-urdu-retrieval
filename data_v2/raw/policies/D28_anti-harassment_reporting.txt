# Anti-Harassment Reporting

Document ID: D28
Policy Area: Anti-Harassment Reporting

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Anti-Harassment Reporting policy outlines the procedure for filing and processing harassment complaints on campus.

A harassment complaint must be filed within 3 days of the incident. Grievance filing after this period is only accepted with compelling justification.

All complaints are handled by the anti-harassment committee and treated with strict confidentiality. The committee must complete its inquiry within thirty days of receiving the report.
