# Course Evaluation

Document ID: D26
Policy Area: Course Evaluation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Course Evaluation policy governs the end-of-semester feedback process for all taught courses.

Course evaluation is fully anonymous. Neither the instructor nor the department can identify individual student responses at any stage.

The feedback survey is open for two weeks before final examinations. Course review results are shared with instructors after grades are officially released.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Course Evaluation policy.
