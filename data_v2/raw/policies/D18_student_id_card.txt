# Student ID Card

Document ID: D18
Policy Area: Student ID Card

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Student ID Card policy defines the procedure and processing timeline for issuing campus access cards.

The card issuance process takes 15 days from the date of application. Students must collect their identity card in person from the registrar's office.

The campus access card is required for library entry, examination halls, and laboratory access. Lost cards must be reported immediately and a replacement fee applies.
