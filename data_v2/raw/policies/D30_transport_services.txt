# Transport Services

Document ID: D30
Policy Area: Transport Services

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Transport Services policy defines the bus service routes and charges for students using university transportation.

The annual transport charges for the campus shuttle are PKR 15,000. The bus service operates on ten fixed routes across the city.

Transport charges are billed with the semester fee. Students may discontinue the campus shuttle service at the end of any semester by submitting a written request.

End of Transport Services policy.
