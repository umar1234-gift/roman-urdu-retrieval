# Official Transcript

Document ID: D19
Policy Area: Official Transcript

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Official Transcript policy specifies the process for obtaining a certified academic record from the university.

The transcript request is processed within 5 working days of submission. Rush processing is available for an additional fee and reduces the timeline to 2 working days.

The academic record is issued in a sealed envelope and cannot be re-opened by the student. Grade reports and transcripts are mailed directly to the requesting institution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Official Transcript policy.
