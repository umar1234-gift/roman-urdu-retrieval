# Annual Sports Day

Document ID: D24
Policy Area: Annual Sports Day

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Annual Sports Day policy defines the schedule and participation rules for the university's athletic events.

The sports gala will be held on 15 March each year. All students are encouraged to register for at least one athletic event.

The annual tournament includes track events, team sports, and individual competitions. Winners receive medals and a cash prize sponsored by the alumni association.

End of Annual Sports Day policy.
