# Semester Fee Structure

Document ID: D09
Policy Area: Semester Fee Structure

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Semester Fee Structure policy describes the tuition payment schedule for enrolled students.

For the fifth semester of the Software Engineering program, the semester charges are PKR 62,500. This tuition payment covers instructional costs, laboratory access, and examination fees.

The fee invoice is issued at the start of each semester. Students must clear their dues before the end of the second week to avoid a late payment surcharge.
