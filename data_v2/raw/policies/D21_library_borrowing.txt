# Library Borrowing

Document ID: D21
Policy Area: Library Borrowing

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Library Borrowing policy defines the loan limits and return periods for library materials.

A student may borrow up to 5 books at a time. The book loan period is fourteen days, renewable once if no other student has placed a hold.

Overdue materials incur a fine of PKR 20 per day per book. Students with outstanding library checkout dues cannot register for the next semester.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

End of Library Borrowing policy.
