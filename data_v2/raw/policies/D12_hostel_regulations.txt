# Hostel Regulations

Document ID: D12
Policy Area: Hostel Regulations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hostel Regulations policy governs the daily conduct and timing rules for students residing in university accommodation.

All residents must return to the hostel by 10:00 PM. The hostel curfew is enforced by the residence warden, and the main gate is locked at this time.

Exceptions for late return require prior written permission from the warden. Students returning after the residence timing without permission face disciplinary action.
