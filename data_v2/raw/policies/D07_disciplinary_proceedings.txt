# Disciplinary Proceedings

Document ID: D07
Policy Area: Disciplinary Proceedings

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Disciplinary Proceedings policy governs the handling of student misconduct and the process for appealing disciplinary decisions.

A student who wishes to contest a disciplinary ruling must file a formal appeal within 10 working days of receiving the official notification. The appeal window is strictly enforced.

Appeals filed after the 10 working day period are rejected without review. The disciplinary committee's decision becomes final once this deadline passes.
