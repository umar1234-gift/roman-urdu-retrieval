# Semester Registration

Document ID: D16
Policy Area: Semester Registration

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Semester Registration policy defines the minimum and maximum course load a student may register for in a semester.

The minimum registration load is 10 credit hours per semester. Students registering below this minimum enrollment are marked as part-time and may face scholarship ineligibility.

The maximum course load is 21 credit hours. Any request to exceed this limit must be approved by the academic advisor and the department chair.

End of Semester Registration policy.
