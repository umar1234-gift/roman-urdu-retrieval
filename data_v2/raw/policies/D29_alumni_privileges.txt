# Alumni Privileges

Document ID: D29
Policy Area: Alumni Privileges

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Alumni Privileges policy describes the benefits available to graduates of the university.

All graduates receive lifetime access to the university library and online journal databases. Alumni benefits also include discounted rates for continuing education programs.

To activate alumni benefits, graduates must register with the alumni office and obtain an alumni card. Graduate services are extended to all degree holders regardless of graduation year.
